The moon shone softly over the quiet town. A clock chimed in the distance, and the streets settled into sleep.
At the edge of the town stood a small library. Its shelves held stories of explorers, inventors, and dreamers.
Every evening, a curious student opened one of those books and wrote down a new question.
Questions became experiments, experiments became lessons, and lessons became new ideas.
Learning is a journey built one careful step at a time.
